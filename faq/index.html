What is Notibrate?

Notibrate gives each app on your phone its own vibration pattern, so you can tell which app is notifying you without looking. For example, WhatsApp could be three short buzzes and email one long buzz.

Why do I feel a double buzz, or an uneven pattern?

The app you chose still has its own vibration switched on, so you feel the app's buzz and Notibrate's pattern at the same time. Open the app's notification settings and switch its vibration off. Notibrate shows you how when you set up a pattern, and you can open the settings again from Notibrate at any time.

If your phone is in Vibrate mode, Android also turns an app's notification sound into an extra buzz, even when that app's vibration is off. To stop this, set the app's notification sound to Silent as well. Notibrate reminds you about this when your phone switches to Vibrate mode.

Why doesn't Notibrate vibrate when my phone is on Silent?

Silent means no sounds and no vibrations, so Notibrate stays quiet too. If you'd like to feel notifications from certain people or apps while everything else is quiet, use your phone's Do Not Disturb mode instead and add them as exceptions: Notibrate vibrates for anything Do Not Disturb lets through.

Why don't I feel patterns in Do Not Disturb?

Notibrate follows your Do Not Disturb settings: it only vibrates for notifications that Do Not Disturb lets through (for example your favourite contacts or the apps you've allowed).

Some phones go further and block every vibration while Do Not Disturb is on, even for the apps you've allowed. Notibrate can't override this. If you don't feel any patterns in Do Not Disturb even for allowed apps, your phone is one of these.

Why did Notibrate stop working?

Usually it's battery saving. To save power, some phones put apps to sleep when they haven't been opened for a while, and a sleeping Notibrate can't notice notifications. To fix it:

  1. In Notibrate, go to Settings › Keep Notibrate running. This opens Notibrate's app info page.
  2. Tap Battery and choose Unrestricted (the names vary between phones).
  3. On Samsung phones, also check that Notibrate isn't listed under Settings › Battery › Background usage limits › Sleeping apps.

If Notibrate's home screen shows "Notibrate isn't receiving notifications", switch Notibrate's notification access off and on again using the button on that message.

Why do I only feel one pattern when several messages arrive at once?

When several notifications arrive from the same app together, Notibrate plays that app's pattern once, so the patterns don't run into each other. If notifications from different apps arrive together, Notibrate plays them one after the other, with a short pause in between. With Cooldown on (Settings), Notibrate also vibrates at most once every 3 seconds for each app.

Does Notibrate read my messages?

No. Notibrate only sees which app sent a notification. It never reads, stores or shares what a notification says. Android shows a standard warning when you give any app notification access; that warning is the same for every app and can be safely ignored for Notibrate.

Why doesn't Notibrate vibrate on my tablet?

Notibrate needs a vibration motor, and many tablets don't have one. Notibrate tells you when it starts if your device can't vibrate.

How do I remove a pattern from an app?

On Notibrate's home screen, tap Reset individual apps, tick the app and tap the tick. Notibrate then reminds you how to switch the app's own vibration (and sound) back on if you'd like it back.
