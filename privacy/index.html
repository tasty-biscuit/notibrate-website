Notibrate privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Notibrate is an Android app made by Notibrate.com, United Kingdom. This policy explains what information Notibrate uses and what happens to it. The short version: Notibrate doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

What Notibrate uses on your phone

  1. Which app sent a notification. With your permission (Android's "notification access"), Notibrate sees which app sent each notification and its type (for example, whether it's a phone call), so it can play the pattern you chose for that app. Notibrate never reads, stores or shares what a notification says.
  2. The list of apps on your phone, so you can choose which apps get a pattern.
  3. Your phone's sound mode and Do Not Disturb setting, so Notibrate stays quiet when it should.

What Notibrate keeps

Notibrate keeps your app-to-pattern choices and its own settings (such as Theme and Cooldown) on your phone only. Notibrate has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it doesn't send any of this information anywhere.

Notibrate's choices and settings are not included in Android's backups or in phone-to-phone transfers, so they never leave your phone. If you move to a new phone, you'll need to set your patterns up again.

Notifications from Notibrate

If you allow it, Notibrate shows a silent reminder when your phone switches to Vibrate mode. These reminders are created on your phone and contain no personal information.

Updates

Notibrate uses Google Play's in-app update feature to tell you when a new version is available. This is provided by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy.

If you contact us

If you email us, for example using Send feedback in the app, we receive your email address and whatever you write. The feedback email the app prepares includes your Notibrate version, phone model and Android version to help with any problems; you can remove these before sending. We only use your email to reply to you and to improve Notibrate, we don't share it, and we delete it within 12 months.

This website

This website (notibrate.com) uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and doesn't load anything from other companies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect its service.

Children

Notibrate doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date.
