Know which app it is, without looking

Notibrate gives each app on your Android phone its own vibration pattern. WhatsApp could be three short buzzes and email one long buzz, so you can tell who's calling for your attention while your phone stays in your pocket.

Why Notibrate?

  • A pattern for every app

    Choose a different vibration for each app you care about, from a short tap to a long buzz.

  • Fits in with your phone

    Notibrate follows your Silent and Do Not Disturb settings, so it stays quiet when it should.

  • Private by design

    Notibrate never reads your messages. It has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
