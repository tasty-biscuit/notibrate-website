Know which app is notifying you, without looking

Notibrate lets you give each app on your Android phone its own vibration pattern: WhatsApp could play three short buzzes, and email one long buzz. You'll know which app needs your attention, even with your phone in your pocket!

Why Notibrate?

  • A pattern for every app

    Choose a different vibration pattern for each app you care about - multiple patterns available, including customisation.

  • Fits in with your phone

    Notibrate follows your Silent and Do Not Disturb settings, so it stays quiet when you need it to.

  • Privacy by design

    Notibrate never reads your messages. It has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.
    Read the privacy policy.

  • Accessible:

    Notibrate has been created with accessibility in mind. It's designed to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA throughout.
